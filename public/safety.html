Child Safety Standards

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

OFG Connects has a zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and the exploitation of children. We strictly prohibit the upload, sharing, or distribution of any content that depicts, promotes, or normalizes child sexual abuse or exploitation.

2. Detection and Removal

We employ a combination of user reporting and moderation tools to identify and remove illegal content. Any content identified as CSAM is immediately removed from our platform and the associated accounts are permanently banned.

3. Reporting to Authorities

In compliance with U.S. law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A) and international standards, OFG Connects will report any suspected instances of CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline. We also cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies in investigating and prosecuting offenders.

4. In-App Reporting

Users are encouraged to report any content they believe violates this policy using the in-app "Report" feature or by contacting us directly. All reports regarding child safety are prioritized for immediate review.
