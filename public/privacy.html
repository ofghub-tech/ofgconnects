Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 30, 2025

1. Introduction

OFG Connects ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information. By using OFG Connects, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our social networking and video sharing features, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you sign in with Google, we collect your Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture to create your account.
  • User Generated Content: We collect and store the photos, videos, and comments you upload to the app.
  • Device Information: We use third-party services (Google AdMob) that may collect device identifiers (like Advertising ID) to serve personalized ads.
  • Audio/Video: We request access to your camera and microphone to allow you to record and upload videos.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Display your videos and posts to the OFG Connects community.
  • Enable social interactions (follows, comments).
  • Display advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Enforce our Community Guidelines and moderate content.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may also collect data:

  • Appwrite: For backend database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Google Sign-In: For secure authentication.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

How to request deletion:

  • In-App: Navigate to Settings > Request Account Deletion.
  • By Email: You may also request deletion by emailing us at ofgtechhub@gmail.com. We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 30 days.

6. Children’s Privacy

OFG Connects is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
